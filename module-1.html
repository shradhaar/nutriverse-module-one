What the 2025–2030 Guidelines Emphasize

Nice work finishing the module. Here’s a quick review of the key ideas.

🥩Protein

Prioritize at every meal from animal and plant sources.

🥛Dairy

Full-fat dairy with no added sugars when you consume it.

🥦Vegetables

Colorful, whole vegetables throughout the day.

🍊Fruits

Whole fruits in their original form when possible.

🫒Healthy Fats

From whole foods and cooking oils like olive oil.

🌾Whole Grains

Fiber-rich grains; limit refined carbohydrates.

  • Choose water and unsweetened beverages for hydration.
  • Limit highly processed packaged foods with added sugars, sodium, and chemical additives.
  • Keep added sugars to no more than 10 grams per meal.
  • Keep sodium under 2,300 mg per day (ages 14+).
  • Support gut health with vegetables, fruits, fermented foods, and high-fiber foods.
