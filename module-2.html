Building a Balanced American Plate

Nice work finishing Module 2. Here’s a quick review of MyPlate.

🥦Vegetables

Fill about half your plate with colorful vegetables.

🍎Fruits

Add whole fruit as part of half your plate.

🌾Grains

Choose whole grains like brown rice, oats, or whole-wheat bread.

🍗Protein

Vary protein: poultry, seafood, eggs, beans, nuts, and lean meats.

🥛Dairy

Include dairy or fortified soy alternatives with meals or snacks.

💧Drinks

Choose water and unsweetened beverages most often.

  • Use USDA MyPlate as a visual guide: vegetables + fruit ≈ half the plate.
  • Make at least half your grains whole grains.
  • Vary your protein foods across the week.
  • Limit highly processed foods, added sugars, and excess sodium.
  • Build realistic American meals — school lunch, home cooking, and restaurant choices can all follow MyPlate.
